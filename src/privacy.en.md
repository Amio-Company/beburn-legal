# BeBurn — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy describes what personal data the **BeBurn** app (iPhone and widgets)
processes, for what purposes, for how long, to whom it may be disclosed and how you can
exercise your rights. It is provided under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679
(GDPR) and the Italian Personal Data Protection Code (Legislative Decree 196/2003, the
"Italian Privacy Code").

## 1. Data controller

The data controller is **Vincenzo Morelli**, who publishes BeBurn on the App Store.

For any question about the processing of your personal data, or to exercise your rights,
you can write to **info@amiocompany.com**.

No Data Protection Officer (DPO) has been appointed, as the law does not require one for
an activity of this kind (Art. 37 GDPR).

## 2. In short

- BeBurn can be used **without an account**: in that case your data stays on your phone
  and is not sent to us. The only exception is the answers you choose to give to BeBurn's
  questions (section 3.3), which reach us without any data that identifies you.
- If you create an account, we keep a copy of your data on our server, in a data centre
  in the European Union, so that it is not lost and you can find it again on another
  iPhone.
- We do not sell your data, show advertising, use analytics or tracking tools, or carry
  out profiling.
- You can delete your account and all your data from the app at any time.

## 3. Data we process

### 3.1 Data that stays only on your iPhone

The following data is saved by the app on your phone (and, for the widgets, in a space
shared between the app and its widgets). **It is never sent to us**, with or without an
account:

- all the data you enter, if you use BeBurn **without an account**;
- **pending Apple Pay payments** received through the shortcut (amount, merchant,
  currency, date and time), until you register or ignore them;
- your **Track** answers (how often you use a subscription or a fixed cost), the daily
  history of your costs, your commute details, the price of your vices, the Home layout
  and your budget;
- the messages written in **Report a problem** or **Send us an idea**, until you send
  them. When you send them, Mail opens with the message already filled in, and it reaches
  us as an email (see section 3.4); the screenshot and the iPhone's technical details are
  attached only if you choose to include them;
- **notifications** (they are local: the app prepares them on your phone), **Face ID or
  Touch ID unlock** (handled by iOS: BeBurn never has access to your face or fingerprint)
  and the data shown in the **widgets**.

To suggest the category of a payment, the app may use the Apple Intelligence model
**running on your iPhone**: the merchant's name does not leave the phone.

You can delete this data with **Profile › App › Erase data and start over** (without an
account), with **Delete account** (with an account) or by uninstalling the app.

### 3.2 Data sent to our server, if you create an account

- **Account data:** your email address; your password, stored only in irreversibly
  encrypted form (bcrypt), which does not allow us to know it; your name, if you enter it
  or share it through Sign in with Apple; a six-digit verification code, valid for 10
  minutes, of which the server keeps only a non-reversible fingerprint.
- **If you sign in with Apple:** the identifier Apple assigns to you for BeBurn, your
  email address (including the anonymous relay address Apple may generate instead of
  yours) and an Apple token, stored in encrypted form, which we use solely to disconnect
  BeBurn from your Apple ID when you delete your account.
- **Data you enter in the app:** the name you want to be called, your monthly net
  salary, working hours and days (including remote-working days), lunch break, the
  "translator" unit (for example a coffee and its price); your assets (home, car…),
  routines, expenses, subscriptions, purchases and vices, each with its name, amount,
  frequency, days, dates, category and other settings of the item (for example "lunch at
  the office").
- **Registered Apple Pay payments:** when you register a payment received through the
  shortcut, it becomes a purchase like any other (name, usually the merchant's, amount,
  date, category). BeBurn never has access to your card number or any other payment
  data: only the Shortcuts automation you set up on your iPhone reads them.
- **Technical security data:** when the app communicates with the server (even without
  an account, to read its configuration, such as the address of this page), the server
  records the IP address, the date and time and the request made. This data is used to
  protect the service (for example, to block repeated attempts to guess a password) and
  to fix faults.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health data or advertising
identifiers. We do not ask for data concerning health, religion, political opinions,
sexual orientation or other special categories of personal data (Art. 9 GDPR). As you
choose the names of your expenses, we ask you not to include information of this kind in
them.

### 3.3 Answers to BeBurn's questions, with or without an account

Every now and then BeBurn asks how you are getting on (the "Got two minutes for us?"
question). Answering is optional, and you can turn the questions off in **Profile › App ›
Questions from BeBurn**. If you answer, your answer is sent to us: the rating from 1 to 5,
the features you picked as most useful, the text you write in "What's missing?" (optional,
200 characters at most), and the date and time of the answer. The same applies to the
answers already given to the first launch questions ("Is it useful?"), which stayed on the
phone.

- **With an account**, the answer is linked to your account.
- **Without an account**, the answer reaches us without a name, email, or any phone or
  installation identifier: we cannot link it to you, or to your other answers.

Please do not write data that identifies you or personal information in "What's
missing?". We use the answers only to understand what works and what to improve in
BeBurn.

### 3.4 Email correspondence

If you email us, we keep your address and the content of your message solely in order to
reply to you.

## 4. Purposes and legal basis of processing

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Create and manage your account, verify your email, let you sign in (including with Apple) | account data | performance of the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) |
| Keep a copy of your data and sync it across your iPhones | data you enter, registered payments | performance of the service (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Delete your account and disconnect Sign in with Apple | Apple token | carrying out your request (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Protect the service from abuse and fix faults | technical security data | our legitimate interest in the security of the service (Art. 6(1)(f) and Recital 49) |
| Understand what works and what to improve in the app | answers to BeBurn's questions | our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f)): you answer only if you want to, and you can turn the questions off |
| Reply to you when you write to us | email and message | carrying out your request (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Comply with legal obligations and defend our rights | the data needed, case by case | legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) or legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) |

BeBurn's calculations (daily cost, Freedom Hour, coffees, working hours) are performed by
the app solely to show them to you. **We do not take decisions based solely on automated
processing** that produce legal or similarly significant effects concerning you, and **we
do not carry out profiling** (Art. 22 GDPR).

To save data on your phone, the app only uses storage that is strictly necessary for the
service you have requested (Art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code): no cookies, no
tracking.

## 5. Whether you are required to provide your data

Providing your data is not mandatory: the app works without an account. If you want an
account, an email address is required, as without it the account cannot be created or
verified. Name and salary are optional; without a salary, however, some calculations,
such as the Freedom Hour, cannot be made.

## 6. Retention

| Data | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Account data and data you enter | for as long as you have the account. When you delete it, the data is erased from the server immediately: we keep no backup copies of the database. |
| Sign-up with an email never verified | 7 days, after which it is deleted automatically |
| Verification code | 10 minutes, or less after 5 failed attempts |
| App session | access lasts 30 minutes and renews automatically; renewal remains valid for at most 30 days if you do not use the app, and ends immediately when you sign out |
| Apple token, if you delete your account and Apple does not respond at that moment | in encrypted form and no longer linked to you, until Apple confirms the disconnection, and in any case for no more than about one month |
| Answers to BeBurn's questions | 2 years from sending, then deleted automatically; with an account, also deleted immediately when you delete the account |
| Technical security data (server logs) | 30 days at most |
| Emails you send us | as long as needed to reply to you and close the request |
| Data kept only on your phone | until you delete it from the app or uninstall BeBurn |

## 7. Recipients of the data

Your data is processed by us. Some providers process it on our behalf, as processors
(Art. 28 GDPR), solely for the purposes described in this policy and under a contract
that requires them to protect it:

- **Railway Corporation** (United States) hosts the server and the database in a data
  centre in the European Union (the Netherlands). Data is also encrypted on disk.
- **Resend** (Plus Five Five, Inc., United States) sends the email with the verification
  code and receives only your email address and the code.

The following act as independent controllers, under their own privacy policies:

- **Apple** (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland), for Sign in with Apple,
  the App Store, any BeBurn Pro subscriptions, Shortcuts, Apple Pay, Apple Intelligence
  and notifications: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>. Subscriptions are managed by
  Apple: we do not receive payment data or your Apple ID, and the app only knows the
  status of the subscription (active or not). If in your iPhone settings you have chosen
  to share analytics with developers, Apple may provide us with aggregate statistics and
  crash reports, which do not identify you.
- **GitHub** (GitHub, Inc.) hosts BeBurn's legal pages, including this one: when you
  view them, GitHub processes your IP address under its own privacy policy.

We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties. We may disclose it to authorities
only where the law requires it.

## 8. Transfer to third countries

The database is located in the European Union, but Railway and Resend are US companies
and may access the data from the United States. In those cases the transfer is subject
to appropriate safeguards: the **Standard Contractual Clauses** approved by the European
Commission (Art. 46 GDPR), included in our contracts with the providers, and, for
certified providers, the **EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework** (adequacy decision of 10 July
2023, Art. 45 GDPR). You can ask us for a copy of these safeguards by writing to the
address in section 1.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access** your data and obtain a copy (Art. 15);
- **rectify** it (Art. 16);
- **obtain its erasure** (Art. 17);
- **obtain restriction** of its processing (Art. 18);
- **receive** it in a machine-readable format and transmit it to another service (data
  portability, Art. 20);
- **object** to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21).

How to exercise them:

- **Access and rectification:** you can view and edit everything you have entered in the
  app (Expenses, Routines, Profile).
- **Erasure:** **Profile › App › Delete account** deletes your account and all your data,
  from the server and from the phone, and disconnects Sign in with Apple. Without an
  account: **Profile › App › Erase data and start over**. Any subscription must be
  cancelled separately, from Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
- **Copy and portability:** **Profile › App › Download my data** generates, free of
  charge and for all users, a JSON file with all the data BeBurn holds about you:
  settings, salary, assets, routines, expenses, subscriptions, purchases, Track answers,
  history, the Apple Pay payments received and your answers to BeBurn's questions; with an account, the file also
  corresponds to the copy held on the server. With BeBurn Pro, **Export data (CSV)** is
  also available, to open your expenses with Numbers or Excel.
- **Answers to BeBurn's questions:** with an account, they are deleted together with the
  account. Those sent without an account are not linked to you and we cannot find them
  (Art. 11 GDPR); they are deleted automatically after 2 years.
- **Other requests** (changing the account email, restriction, objection): write to the
  address in section 1. We will reply within one month (Art. 12 GDPR); to verify your
  identity, we may ask you to write from the email address linked to the account.

You can withdraw iOS permissions (notifications, Face ID) at any time from Settings ›
BeBurn.

If you believe that the processing of your data does not comply with the law, you can
lodge a complaint with the Italian **Garante per la protezione dei dati personali**
(Piazza Venezia 11, 00187 Rome, <https://www.garanteprivacy.it>) or with the supervisory
authority of the European country where you live or work (Art. 77 GDPR).

## 10. Children

BeBurn is not intended for anyone under 14 and we do not knowingly collect data from
children under 14. If you believe that a child under 14 has provided us with their data,
write to us and we will delete it.

## 11. Security

We protect data with technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk
(Art. 32 GDPR): always-encrypted connections (HTTPS); passwords hashed with bcrypt;
short-lived session tokens, stored on the phone in the iOS Keychain and on the server
only in non-reversible form; encrypted Apple tokens; database encrypted on disk; each
user can access only their own data; limits on sign-in and verification attempts. On your
phone you can also enable Face ID or Touch ID unlock and hide the figures with the eye
button.

In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify the Garante within 72 hours and,
if the breach is likely to result in a high risk to you, we will also inform you
(Articles 33 and 34 GDPR).

## 12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. If the change is significant
(for example new data being collected or a new provider), we will inform you in the app
before it applies.
