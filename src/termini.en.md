## 3. What the service does

BeBurn turns the expenses you enter (assets, routines, subscriptions, purchases, vices)
into a daily cost and compares it with what you earn: the cost of the day, the Freedom
Hour, hours of work, coffees. The results are **estimates**, based solely on the data you
enter and on simplified formulas. If the data is incomplete or incorrect, so are the
results.
