## 2. What BeBurn does

BeBurn turns the expenses you enter (assets, routines, subscriptions, purchases, vices) into a
daily cost and compares it with what you earn: the cost of the day, the Freedom Hour, hours of
work, coffees. These are **estimates**: they are based only on the data you type in and on
simplified formulas. If the data is incomplete or wrong, so are the results.
