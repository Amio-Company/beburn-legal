What we collect

BeBurn requires an account. On our server we keep:

  • account data: email and password (hashed only, unreadable even to us). If you sign in with Apple: the identifier Apple gives us, your email (including the private relay address Apple can create for you), your name if you choose to share it, and an Apple token we only use to disconnect the sign-in when you delete your account;
  • what you enter in the app: net salary, working hours and days, expenses, subscriptions, purchases, routines, assets and the simulations you save;
  • Apple Pay payments you log through the shortcut: amount and merchant. BeBurn never sees your card details;
  • install information: the app version you first downloaded and its date, to recognise early users.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising data. We use no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

These stay on your phone only and never reach us: Face ID or Touch ID unlock (handled by iOS), notifications and the data shown in widgets.

Why we use it

Only to run BeBurn: working out what each day costs you, keeping a copy of your data so you don't lose it and find it on another phone, signing you in and sending you the email verification code. The legal basis is providing the service you ask for (art. 6.1.b GDPR).

We don't sell your data and we don't use it to profile you.

Pro features
