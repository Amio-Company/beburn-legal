Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the BeBurn app (iPhone and widgets) processes, why, for how long, who we share it with and how you can exercise your rights. It is provided under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the Italian Personal Data Protection Code (Legislative Decree 196/2003).

1. Who processes your data

The data controller is Vincenzo Morelli, who publishes BeBurn on the App Store.

For any privacy question, or to exercise your rights, write to info@amiocompany.com.

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO): for an activity like ours the law does not require one.

2. In short

  • BeBurn works without an account too: in that case your data stays on your phone and we never receive it.
  • If you create an account, we keep a copy of your data on our server, in a data centre in the European Union, so you don't lose it and can find it again on another iPhone.
  • We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, we don't use analytics or tracking tools, and we don't profile you.
  • You can delete your account and all your data from the app, at any time.

3. What data we process

3.1 Data that stays only on your iPhone

The app saves this data on your phone (and, for the widgets, in a space shared between the app and its widgets). It never reaches us, with or without an account:

  • everything you enter, if you use BeBurn without an account;
  • pending Apple Pay payments you receive through the shortcut (amount, merchant, currency, date and time) until you register or ignore them;
  • your Track answers (how often you use a subscription or a fixed cost), the daily history of your costs, your commute details, the price of your vices, the Home layout, your budget;
  • your answers to the launch questions ("Is it useful?", the 10-day rating) and the messages written in Report a problem or Send us an idea until you send them: when you do, Mail opens with the message already written and it reaches us as an email (see section 3.3), with the screenshot and the iPhone's technical details only if you choose to attach them;
  • notifications (they are local: the app prepares them on your phone), Face ID or Touch ID unlock (handled by iOS: BeBurn never sees your face or fingerprint) and the data shown in the widgets.

To suggest the category of a payment, the app may use the Apple Intelligence model running on your iPhone: the merchant's name never leaves the phone.

You delete this data with Profile › App › Erase data and start over (without an account), with Delete account (with an account) or by uninstalling the app.

3.2 Data that reaches our server, if you create an account

  • Account data: your email address; your password, only in irreversibly encrypted form (bcrypt: not even we know it); your name, if you type it or share it through Sign in with Apple; a six-digit verification code, valid for 10 minutes (the server keeps only a non-reversible fingerprint of it).
  • If you sign in with Apple: the identifier Apple assigns to you for us, your email (including the anonymous relay address Apple may create instead of yours) and an Apple token, stored encrypted, which we only use to disconnect BeBurn from your Apple ID when you delete your account.
  • Data you enter in the app: the name you want to be called, your monthly net salary, working hours and days (including remote-working days), lunch break, the "translator" unit (for example a coffee and its price); your assets (home, car…), routines, expenses, subscriptions, purchases and vices, each with its name, amount, frequency, days, dates, category and other settings of the item (for example "lunch at the office").
  • Registered Apple Pay payments: when you register a payment received through the shortcut, it becomes a purchase like any other (name, usually the merchant's, amount, date, category). BeBurn never sees your card number or any other payment data: only the Shortcuts automation you set up on your iPhone reads them.
  • Technical security data: when the app talks to the server (even without an account, to read its configuration, such as the address of this page), the server records the IP address, date and time and the request made. This protects the service (for example, it stops repeated password guessing) and helps us fix faults.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, health data or advertising identifiers. We don't ask for data about health, religion, political opinions, sexual orientation or other "special categories" (Art. 9 GDPR). You choose the names of your expenses: please don't put information of this kind in them.

3.3 If you write to us

If you email us, we keep your address and what you tell us, only to answer you.

5. Do you have to give us your data?

No. The app works without an account. If you want an account, an email address is required (without it we can't create or verify it). Name and salary are optional: without a salary some calculations, such as the Freedom Hour, can't be made.

7. Who receives the data

We process your data ourselves. Some providers help us, processing it on our behalf, only for the purposes of this policy, under a contract that requires them to protect it (processors, Art. 28 GDPR):

  • Railway Corporation (United States) hosts the server and the database, in a data centre in the European Union (the Netherlands). Data is also encrypted on disk.
  • Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc., United States) sends the email with the verification code: it only receives your email address and the code.

The following are independent controllers, with their own policies:

  • Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland), for Sign in with Apple, the App Store, any BeBurn Pro subscriptions, Shortcuts, Apple Pay, Apple Intelligence and notifications: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/. Subscriptions are handled by Apple: we don't receive payment data or your Apple ID; the app only knows whether the subscription is active. If in your iPhone settings you chose to share analytics with developers, Apple may give us aggregate statistics and crash reports that don't tell us who you are.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc.) hosts BeBurn's legal pages, including this one: when you open them, GitHub processes your IP address under its own policy.

We don't sell or hand over your data to anyone. We may disclose it to authorities only where the law requires it.

8. Transfers outside the European Union

The database is in the European Union, but Railway and Resend are US companies and may access the data from the United States. In those cases the transfer is protected by the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission (Art. 46 GDPR), included in our contracts with the providers, and, for certified providers, by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of 10 July 2023, Art. 45 GDPR). You can ask us for a copy of these safeguards at the address in section 1.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy (Art. 15);
  • rectify it (Art. 16);
  • erase it (Art. 17);
  • restrict its processing (Art. 18);
  • receive it in a machine-readable format and transmit it to another service (data portability, Art. 20);
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21).

How:

  • See and correct: you see and edit everything you entered in the app (Expenses, Routines, Profile).
  • Erase: Profile › App › Delete account deletes your account and all your data, from the server and from the phone, and disconnects Sign in with Apple. Without an account: Profile › App › Erase data and start over. Any subscription is cancelled separately, from Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
  • Get a copy and export: Profile › App › Download my data creates, free and for everyone, a JSON file with everything BeBurn keeps about you: settings, salary, assets, routines, expenses, subscriptions, purchases, Monitor answers, history and the Apple Pay payments received; with an account it is also the copy of what is on the server. With BeBurn Pro there is also Export data (CSV), to open your expenses with Numbers or Excel.
  • Everything else (changing the account email, restriction, objection): write to the address in section 1. We answer within one month (Art. 12 GDPR); we may ask you to write from the account's email, to make sure it's you.

You can withdraw iOS permissions (notifications, Face ID) at any time from Settings › BeBurn.

If you believe we process your data unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (Piazza Venezia 11, 00187 Rome, https://www.garanteprivacy.it) or with the authority of the European country where you live or work.

10. Children

BeBurn is not meant for anyone under 14 and we don't knowingly collect data from children under 14. If you believe a child under 14 has given us their data, write to us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We protect data with measures appropriate to the risk (Art. 32 GDPR): always-encrypted connections (HTTPS); passwords hashed with bcrypt; short-lived session tokens, stored on the phone in the iOS Keychain and on the server only in non-reversible form; encrypted Apple tokens; database encrypted on disk; each user sees only their own data; limits on sign-in and verification attempts. On your phone you can add Face ID or Touch ID unlock and hide the figures with the eye button.

If a data breach occurred, we would notify the Garante within 72 hours and, if the risk to you were high, you as well (Articles 33 and 34 GDPR).

12. Changes

If we change this policy we update the date at the top. If the change is significant (for example new data collected or a new provider), we tell you in the app before it applies.
